# Recurring patterns across eras

## 1. Fight sparsity with external knowledge
Whenever interaction data is scarce, recommender systems turn to external knowledge. In different eras this takes the form of constraints, metadata, linked data, knowledge graphs, or natural-language descriptions.

## 2. Keep reasons communicable
Explainability is not just presentation polish. Systems repeatedly benefit from representations that users can question, inspect, and revise.

## 3. Push heavy enrichment offline
From side-information factorization to graph preprocessing to LLM teacher-student distillation, the field repeatedly separates expensive enrichment from the latency-critical serving path.

## 4. Hybridize instead of replace
Rules were not fully replaced by features; graphs did not erase embeddings; LLMs do not make retrieval or structured knowledge obsolete. Robust systems compose these layers.
