# Research questions

## RQ1 — Representational translations
How has external knowledge shifted over two decades, from rules and constraints to feature matrices, structured graphs, embeddings, and natural language, and what capabilities were gained or obscured at each translation?

## RQ2 — Recurring design patterns
What design patterns repeatedly reappear across eras, even when the model family changes? The paper highlights four: fighting sparsity with external knowledge, making reasons communicable, separating offline enrichment from online decision-making, and hybridizing instead of replacing.

## RQ3 — Responsible knowledge integration
What risks appear when recommendation becomes generative and conversational? The paper connects modern concerns such as hallucination, prompt-sensitive fairness, privacy leakage, and weak reproducibility to older RecSys goals like validity, traceability, and user protection.
